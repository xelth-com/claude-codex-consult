## Framing
The brief assumes usefulness ratings, lab labels and panel size are adequate proxies for review value. Missing alternatives: deterministic stratified rotation; Thompson/UCB routing; scoring verified-finding precision instead of judge satisfaction; diversity based on observed correlated errors or endpoint groups; operator-pinned members plus automatic fill; and budget-aware selection using cost, latency and quota. I would choose only after knowing rating volume/sparsity, its correlation with structured replies, panel budgets, cost/latency distributions, correlated failures by lab/endpoint, and whether the objective is defect discovery, independent consensus or reliable acceptance.

## Q1
Use a confidence-weighted rate, not the additive sum. For example `(yes + 0.5*partly + 2)/(rated + 4)` or a Beta posterior lower bound, hierarchically shrunk from all-purpose to purpose/topic evidence. The proposed sum rewards exposure without normalizing and its floor collapses all negative histories. Keep recency decay rather than a hard 90-day cliff.

## Q2
Exploration 0.2 per slot is plausible only cold: for four slots it expects 0.8 explorations and gives about 60% of panels at least one. It becomes expensive once evidence accumulates. Decay it with effective sample size or use posterior sampling; cap exploratory slots at one per panel.

## Q3
Yes. If lab A has scores 10 and 9.5 while lab B has 0.25, size 2 selects 10 and 0.25 instead of 10 and 9.5. Make diversity a soft bonus or reserve one seat only when candidates' confidence intervals overlap or scores are within a documented tolerance.

## Q4
Keep roles prompt-only. `codex-consult.ps1:713-735,936-939` already gives purpose presets and explicit overrides; silently changing effort or words by role would confound comparisons and telemetry. Permit explicit `-Effort`/`-MaxWords` as today.

## Q5
Judge quota/auth outages at the shared endpoint-group level, then project the state onto every affected roster entry. Display compact groups such as `gemini :: m1,m2 (until ...)` rather than provider-only rows or ambiguous `*`; show `all N reviewers available` otherwise. This preserves one line without losing actionable identity.
